# Decision modes

The eight modes Rhizome's decision form offers. Pick the mode from the question
and its number of options: the product suggests one by count, but a mode that
doesn't fit the question is wrong even with the right count. The docs page per
mode is under *Funktionen → Entscheidungsmodi* (`/de/funktionen/entscheidungsmodi/`).

| Mode | DE | EN | Options (product's suggestion) | People respond with | Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `consent` | Konsent | Consent | exactly 1 | consent, objection, rarely veto, step aside | an operational or process choice: "good enough for now, safe enough to try" |
| `consensus` | Konsens | Consensus | exactly 1; everyone takes part | agreement, objection, rarely veto, step aside | fundamental matters: values, pay model, the organisation's identity |
| `resistance` | Widerstandsabfrage | Resistance poll | 2 (up to 4) | resistance to every option, 1 = least; step aside | a contentious choice; finds the most widely acceptable option |
| `majority` | Mehrheitsentscheid | Majority vote | 3–4 | one vote; step aside | clearly distinct alternatives, when speed matters |
| `dots` | Punkteverteilung | Dot poll | 5–6 | points, 10 per person by default; step aside | prioritising, splitting a budget |
| `ranking` | Rangfolge | Ranking | 7+ | every option ranked; step aside | order of implementation, long lists |
| `advice` | Konsultation | Insight process | exactly 1 | the decider asks for insights („Perspektive“), then decides with a reason | a role owns the call, but the expertise is spread |
| `role_based` | Rollenbasierte Entscheidung | Role-based | exactly 1 | clarifying questions („Verständnisfrage“); the decider decides with a reason | clearly in someone's remit, and fast |

Hard limits: consent and consensus take exactly one option, the voting modes
at least two. Advice and role-based name one person who decides; the others
are decided together.

- **An objection** (consent, consensus) says how the proposal harms the shared
  goal, ideally with a fix – not a preference ("I'd prefer Thursday" is a
  post). Its usual sequel is a new version of the option, after which everyone
  responds again.
- **A veto** blocks until the person who raised it lifts it. It is rare and
  heavy.
- **Step aside** is visible, carries a reason, and counts toward the quorum.
- **Results are not secret**: names show next to votes, points and ranks.
- A binding result is usually followed by a task – otherwise nobody carries it
  out – and then by solving the topic.
